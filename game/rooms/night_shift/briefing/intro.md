# Night Shift

**23:10, Kestrel Logistics regional office.**

You are contractors finishing a server migration in the sysadmin's office. Ten minutes ago the building's badge system crashed, the mains went with it, and the office maglock slammed shut behind you.

Dale, the night security guard, is on the other side of the building. He can talk to you over the intercom, but he can't get to you. The generator is failing and the UPS has about an hour left in it.

Find the maglock override before the UPS dies.
